## Article brief template

Each article must define:

- primary query and search intent;
- target reader and decision;
- original evidence or point of view;
- official sources checked and date checked;
- outline and key examples;
- related service, guide and case-study links;
- one appropriate CTA;
- author and reviewer;
- publish date, review date and change log.

## First recommended linkable asset

### Automation Opportunity Assessment

User inputs:

- workflow name;
- frequency;
- handling time;
- delay or error cost;
- input quality;
- exception rate;
- systems involved;
- consequence of an incorrect action;
- ability to measure within 30–60 days.

Output:

- a transparent priority summary;
- reasons the workflow may or may not be a good pilot;
- required human checkpoints;
- questions to answer before implementation;
- optional email or WhatsApp handoff.

Why first: it is directly relevant to the service, useful without a sales call, internally linkable from several articles, and more defensible than another generic AI guide.
