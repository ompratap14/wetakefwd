# 90-day SEO roadmap

## Scoring method

Priorities combine business relevance, commercial intent, expected lead value, current evidence, ranking opportunity and implementation effort. They are directional until Search Console and keyword-tool data are added.

## Weeks 1–2 — measurement and recrawl

- Request homepage recrawl in Search Console.
- Export 3-month and 6-month Search Console data for both domains.
- Verify GA4/GTM page-view and conversion events.
- Record a baseline: clicks, impressions, non-brand clicks, qualified leads and top landing pages.
- Confirm sitemap status after the next deployment.
- Add approved team/founder identity and a truthful role description.

Success condition: reliable baseline data and no obsolete branded snippet.

## Weeks 3–4 — commercial page depth

- Improve AI automation, AI development, AI agent, chatbot, business automation and custom software pages.
- Add relevant real work, practical workflow examples and two supporting-content links to each page.
- Clarify the output of the first workflow review.
- Add conversion events for form success, WhatsApp, email and phone.
- Review titles of pages with impressions but low CTR.

Success condition: every core page has distinct intent, proof, supporting content and one measurable CTA.

## Weeks 5–6 — original proof

- Obtain permission for case-study metrics and client attribution links.
- Add project dates, scope, user group and one measured outcome where available.
- Publish behind-the-build articles for DPS/Lokman and SS Cafe.
- Optimise the largest case-study images after visual review.

Success condition: at least two case studies contain client-approved, verifiable operating evidence.

## Weeks 7–8 — linkable asset

- Build the Automation Opportunity Assessment.
- Publish a methodology page explaining the scoring.
- Connect the asset to AI automation guides and services.
- Track assessment starts, completions and lead handoffs.
- Pitch the asset to five relevant publications or communities only after QA.

Success condition: a useful public asset generates engagement or qualified conversations independent of a sales pitch.

## Weeks 9–10 — cluster execution

- Publish four high-priority articles from the calendar.
- Add reciprocal internal links across the relevant pillar.
- Refresh the existing WhatsApp and AI cost articles with dated official sources.
- Compare actual Search Console queries with the keyword map.

Success condition: each priority pillar has at least three strong supporting resources and no intent overlap.

## Weeks 11–12 — authority and outreach

- Complete two client-link requests.
- Submit one vetted AI ecosystem profile.
- Pitch one original guest article.
- Run or propose one useful local/industry workshop.
- Distribute each resource through LinkedIn with a point of view, not only a link.

Success condition: new relevant referring domains or genuine editorial conversations.

## Week 13 — review and reprioritise

- Compare the last 28 days with the preceding 28 days.
- Review query clusters, pages in positions 5–20, CTR and conversions.
- Improve the strongest opportunity page rather than automatically adding pages.
- Archive or consolidate content only when evidence shows overlap or no useful audience.
- Set the next 90-day plan.

## Top ten next actions

1. Export Search Console query and page data for both domains.
2. Request homepage recrawl and monitor the old snippet.
3. Verify GA4/GTM conversion events end to end.
4. Strengthen the six core commercial pages with proof and supporting links.
5. Publish accurate founder/team information.
6. Obtain client-approved metrics and attribution links.
7. Build the Automation Opportunity Assessment.
8. Publish the first four experience-led articles in the calendar.
9. Optimise large case-study images and check mobile Core Web Vitals.
10. Begin a small, personalised backlink outreach cycle.

## What not to do

- Do not publish dozens of city pages.
- Do not target “company” or “agency” language as a brand identity when that description is inaccurate.
- Do not invent traffic, ROI, accuracy, projects, reviews or team credentials.
- Do not purchase manipulative backlinks.
- Do not rewrite the design during an SEO cycle without evidence that design blocks usability or conversion.
- Do not treat indexing as the same as ranking.
