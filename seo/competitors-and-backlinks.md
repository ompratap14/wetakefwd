# Competitor and backlink plan

Research date: 28 November 2026

## Competitor patterns

These are search competitors for relevant queries, not businesses WeTakeFWD should copy.

| Competitor | Observed strength | Opportunity for WeTakeFWD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lets Automation | Founder identity, a concrete free audit, detailed service architecture, written plan and measured claims | Publish a real team profile, define the output of the first review, and use only client-approved metrics |
| TrinityAI | Deep service page organised by problems, features, process, industries, integrations and FAQs | Add more specific workflow and integration detail to the six core commercial pages |
| Trellient | Narrow SMB positioning, audit-first framework, baseline and monthly measurement | Own the practical small-town and non-technical-business perspective using real experience |
| Doomple | Broad service architecture, agent workflows, industry coverage and large content footprint | Stay more focused; win with clearer proof and less generic breadth |
| Velozity | Direct agent-use cases by buyer role and system | Add buyer-role sections to the AI agent page where supported |
| Wonkrew | Clear pricing signal, permissions, comparisons and case results | Explain ownership and controls; publish results only after permission and measurement |

## Strategic position

WeTakeFWD should not compete by claiming the largest AI stack. The defensible position is:

- practical digital and automation work for Indian businesses;
- experience with schools, hospitality and small-town operations;
- one workflow first;
- human oversight and measurable operating outcomes;
- websites, applications and automation connected as one business system.

## Outreach angles

### Client attribution

“We documented the project accurately with real screenshots. If you are comfortable, could your website’s credits or news page link to the case study? We will use the client name and outcome only as approved.”

### Guest contribution

Pitch one original lesson, not a service advertisement. Strong examples:

- what schools, restaurants and shops in small-town India taught us about useful automation;
- how to choose one workflow for an automation pilot;
- why WhatsApp is the operating layer for many Indian small businesses;
- what a human checkpoint should look like in an AI workflow.

### Resource outreach

After the assessment is live, contact publications that already explain small-business operations or AI adoption. Show the method and invite feedback. Do not ask for a link before proving the resource is useful.

## Monthly backlink plan

| Week | Activity | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Request two approved client attribution links | 2 personal requests |
| 2 | Pitch one original guest article and one local workshop | 2 tailored pitches |
| 3 | Improve one owned profile and submit one vetted directory | 2 completed profiles |
| 4 | Promote one linkable asset to relevant editors or communities | 5 highly relevant contacts |

Track replies, placements, referral visits and qualified leads. A smaller number of relevant links is more valuable than a large number of unrelated listings.
