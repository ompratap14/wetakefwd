## Cannibalisation controls

- `/ai-automation-services-india` owns commercial AI automation intent. The guide and Insights articles answer informational questions and link back to it.
- `/ai-agent-development` owns commercial agent intent. `/guides/ai-agent-vs-chatbot` owns the comparison query.
- `/ai-chatbot-development` owns general chatbot development. `/services/whatsapp-crm-automation` owns WhatsApp workflow and CRM intent.
- `/custom-software-development` owns broad custom software. CRM, ERP, API, app and web pages own their narrower functional intents.
- Noindex local pages must not be used as doorway pages. Promote a location page only after it contains distinct proof and serves a genuine market.

## Data-enrichment workflow

1. Export Search Console queries and pages for the last 3 and 6 months.
2. Add impressions, clicks, CTR and average position to this map.
3. Use Google Ads Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush or another approved tool for search-volume ranges and difficulty.
4. Record tool name, country, date and match type.
5. Reprioritise by qualified-lead value and ranking opportunity, not volume alone.
